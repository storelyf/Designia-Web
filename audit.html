      Frequently asked questions

      About this audit

      What is GEO, and how is it different from SEO?

      GEO (generative engine optimization) means getting your business into the answers ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Copilot give when someone asks about a service like yours. SEO works to get you into Google's list of results; GEO works to get the assistant to name you and cite you as a source. They support each other: a well-structured website serves both.

      What do you do with my domain?

      We only read public pages of your site (the homepage, robots.txt, llms.txt and sitemap.xml), using a crawler identified as DesigniaAuditBot. We don't ask for logins or personal data, and we don't store your information: the result is cached for three minutes so we don't read your site twice in a row.

      Does a high score guarantee that AI will recommend me?

      No. The score measures whether a model can read and understand you, which is the prerequisite. Whether it chooses you also depends on your Google Business Profile, your reviews and having content that answers real questions. That's what we work on after the audit.

      Is it really free?

      Yes. The audit is free and requires no sign-up. If you want us to fix what came up short, we chat on WhatsApp and tell you what it involves and what it costs before we start.
